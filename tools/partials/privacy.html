2. What is collected, and why

Artwork inquiries

When you send an inquiry about a work, the form collects your name, email address, your message, and the title of the artwork you asked about.

This is used only to answer you and, if you go ahead, to arrange the sale. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, the form collects your email address, used only to send occasional news about new works and exhibitions. The legal basis is your consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any newsletter or writing to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}, and withdrawing it does not affect anything sent beforehand.

Server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records standard technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That processing is GitHub's, under GitHub's privacy statement. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in serving a working, secure website.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. There is no Google Analytics or any other analytics, no advertising pixels, no social media trackers, and no profiling of any kind. Fonts are served from this site's own server, so no third party is contacted to render the page. Because nothing but strictly necessary technical processing takes place, no cookie banner is required.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access. Get a copy of the personal data held about you.
  • Rectification. Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Erasure. Have your data deleted, where no legal duty requires keeping it.
  • Restriction. Ask that processing be limited while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability. Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection. Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdrawal of consent. At any time, for the newsletter.

To exercise any of these, write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. You will get an answer within one month.

If you believe your data is being handled unlawfully, you have the right to complain to the Swedish supervisory authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), Box 8114, 104 20 Stockholm. See imy.se.

7. Security

This site is served over HTTPS, so everything you send is encrypted in transit. Inquiries are received in an email account protected by a strong password and two-factor authentication. No payment card details are ever collected through this website.
